## Behaviours to reproduce

- **Reconnect scan/order** (`P:279–309`): skip recovering allocations, exhausted/retired devices and removed **and disposed** devices. Then classify disconnected executor before device-lost before skipped-removed/readded. Mark all recoveries before sending Disconnected directly through the shared queue, then launch worker groups under monitoring. This direct send differs from 5.5a's launched notifications. Group disconnected devices by transport; recover each connected-lost device separately, incrementing its persistent signed loss count by Apple ID. Readded devices get `setUpAgain` without a new disconnect or loss increment. The 16 ms loop continues while teardown waits, detecting other workers; it must not await each recovery.
- **Recovery barriers/budget** (`:333–368,680–681,913–923`): dispose/join all devices of a worker concurrently, await that whole phase, then concurrently wait for their parsing sessions to close, **70 s** each (4.8 close timeout + 10 s); false retires the device. Connected losses 1/2/3 pause **0/30/60 s after teardown/session waits**, then provision; loss 4 tears down and logs refusal without provisioning. Losses are not reset by successful replacement, removal/readdition or later connection loss. Worker-connection loss has no loss-budget increment/backoff. Recheck retired, unadmitted/cutoff, and removed immediately before forming UDIDs; record `skippedRemoved` for excluded removals. Provision even the empty set through the existing no-work path. Cancellation rethrows; ordinary errors log `Failed to re-provision simulators of <transport>`; finally clear recovery membership. `setUpAgain` (`:315–326`) logs, provisions the old UDID, and always clears membership/retains a replaced predecessor.
- **Updates and replacement leases** (`:408–422,748–759,867–886`): dead-worker removal leaves disposal to recovery; an update cannot provision a competing recovering/retired/exhausted/removed allocation. Planning re-reads current identity after awaits. Registration rechecks predecessor retirement/exhaustion and removal under the existing lock, including removal during setup. Readdition during a dropped replacement is remembered and retried after recovery clears. Preserve 5.5a's predecessor-dispose-before-Connected ordering and retained leases. Connected-loss recovery retains replaced references in its finally (`:367`); worker-loss recovery does **not** (candidate A90 below), so do not generalize this silently.
- **Pending workers** (`PendingWorker.kt:9–38`, `P:452–501`): ordered target list, duplicates retained for profile multiplicity. Offer appends only while open; take drains a copy, closing only on an empty take; close refuses offers but does not erase stored targets. Atomically offer into an existing pending entry or install one and launch a single retry. Added targets use that entry; a closed/removed entry permits a new first attempt. Finally close and remove only that same pending object (`:624–627`), never a successor.
- **Failure taxonomy/retry** (`:458–490,552–623`): initial attempt counts as 1. Retry only transient failures and only if `pause_after(1)` exists; missing transient setups also enter retry. Reuse the merged classifier from `B/device/ConnectionFailures.kt:27–38`: outer UnreachableHost required, then up to 16 causes, not a blanket I/O retry. Keep A16 (failed key exchange can be transient); A15's approved fast authentication failure stands. Before each retry: compute pause, stop if absent, log periodic mode at `attempt == attempts`, delay, check cutoff, increment, connect. Do not reset attempt after a connection or failed setup. Two provider retry permits cover **connection acquisition only**, inside 4.3's eight handshake slots; initial connections and simulator setup do not take these two permits. Release the planning reference in finally, including cutoff. Filter only removed explicit Simulator targets from each pending take; an empty filtered list must not plan/shutdown unused simulators. Drain additions on the same connection; offer only missing setups back before reconnecting. Keep 5.5a's failure-to-target mapping and fresh provisioning context per setup call.
- **Defaults** (`WorkerConnectionConfiguration.kt:21–59`): attempts 3, backoff 10 s, interval 60 s, lateJoin true, cutoff 10 min. Instant failing attempts occur at scheduler offsets **0,10,30,90,150… seconds**, until cutoff/termination; lateJoin=false ends after attempt 3. It does not disable the first two retries or their admission checks. Reuse the config port's approved overflow saturation, not a new duration implementation.
- **Detached acquisition** (`Detached.kt:15–23`, `P:133–135,575–576,630–635`): cancellation returns promptly without waiting for blocked worker **or per-simulator** connect; a successful undelivered result is released exactly once, including completion-versus-cancellation races. No setup, event or lease transfer after cancellation. Retain the underlying task and release capability until it finishes; provider termination must not call an unbounded `ConnectionFactory::join_pending` over blocked acquisitions. Distinguish this exception from recovery/session teardown that Kotlin does join. Q2 audits the existing acknowledgment seam.
- **Cutoff/admission** (`P:643–673,845–866,902`; `D:313–321,393–399`): cutoff uses wall Timer elapsed **strictly greater than** configured milliseconds; zero disables only that time limit. Any registered device waiting **strictly more than 30 s** by monotonic nanos closes late join globally, across pools. Once observed, the reason stays latched even if work returns or wall time moves backward. Check after retry delay, after connection, after setup at registration, and after prepare before requesting the first batch. First-attempt healthy arrivals are not automatically late: late means pending transport and no existing ID, or already awaiting admission. Replacement before admission inherits the gate; accepted admission clears the ID. Cutoff at setup retires the predecessor and throws `SetupDropped`; a reason from the one-shot prepare closure retires the new device and returns `VendorError::LeavePool` for `NotAdmitted`, never the 30-attempt preparation retry. Lost unadmitted devices past cutoff retire without reprovisioning; admitted devices recover normally.
- **Pool/termination** (`C/execution/DevicePoolActor.kt:131–150,263–299`; `Scheduler.kt:74–127`; `P:776–813`): last-device loss stops a pool after terminating devices account for their batches and the queue barrier completes; recovery is not a reason to keep it alive. Scheduler retains the closed pool; do not resurrect it on Connected. Keep C27 serial-only stale disconnect behavior. Termination cancels/joins monitoring and recoveries, then the retry root, closes the buffered event channel, drains 4.9 artifacts, disposes/releases current devices, and releases retained predecessors. Preserve C25/C26 and 5.5a's disposal-error boundary; do not turn successful parse into provider cleanup.
- **Logs** (`P:317–322,345,357,455,466–488,559–616,664–676,850–875`): keep levels and exact templates, provider logger `AppleSimulatorProvider`, `describe()`'s four causes with nullable-message/simple-class fallback, and the late-worker Timings line. Timing subtraction wraps; its suffix takes a separate Timer read. Use jcompat/Kotlin transport formatting, never Rust Debug; D12 omits JVM artifacts, not custom messages.

**Bug accounting:** preserve A1 (dynamic source EOF stops permanently even while device recovery continues), A16, A50–A52, C25–C27 and any confirmed 5.5a A80–A84 at their seams; A53 and A15 retain their approved deviations. **Reserve A90–A94**. Candidate **A90**: old device leases replaced by *worker-loss* recovery are omitted from both `replaced` insertion sites (`P:367,883–884`), unlike connected-loss replacements; termination only traverses current/replaced devices. Obtain a release-count witness using the real connection factory, then record the reproduced behavior, not an assumed fix. A91–A94 remain available; do not invent rows or mark source-only suspicions confirmed.

## Proposed simplifications (for the orchestrator, not decided)

Until approved, source behavior stands. Existing 5.5a P1/P2 and config overflow decisions already apply.

- **P1 — no generic Detached utility:** satisfy its two production call sites through 4.3's retained acquisition adapter, with the Q2 race correction if needed, rather than porting a general arbitrary-blocking-call executor. No user-visible difference intended; cancellation and eventual release witnesses are mandatory.
- **P2 — first observed cutoff reason wins atomically:** Kotlin's volatile read/check/write can let concurrent callers overwrite one reason with another (`P:645–654`). Use one atomic latch; a simultaneous time/idle or two-idle-device race may log a different winning reason. Keep the strict thresholds, global scope and all admission checks. No JVM map/volatile race emulation solely for competing diagnostic text.

No simplification is proposed for default retry, recovery limits, late admission, last-device pool termination, retained artifact leases or the A90 candidate.
