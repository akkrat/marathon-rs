## Proposed simplifications (orchestrator approval, not decided)

Until approved, reproduce the source behaviour above; approved differences need recorded decisions and retained/marked oracle witnesses.

- **P1 — trim dead external surface.** Keep the cheap command-builder options covered by all ten adam tests, but port only constants from the contract library, without its unused on-device helpers or a general test-access client/server. Protobuf omission is already approved in 6.3. No marathon-visible behaviour changes; retain tested `-f` serialization without adding a host-side output-log service.
- **P2 — failed-attempt cleanup.** Unregister remote log accumulators on every exit and always cancel/join owned instrumentation producers even if afterTestRun throws. Changes resource state/log routing after parsing failure or a failing listener; no success-path argv changes. This can affect ordinary retries, so requires explicit approval and JVM leak witnesses, not an assumed safety exception.
- **P3 — exotic argument arithmetic.** Reject malformed or overflowing emulator-console suffixes and console+3000 overflow with one clear error instead of omission/wrapping. Only abnormal `emulator-*` serials differ; preserve valid ports, all eight names, override precedence and exact commands. Keeping Kotlin may be simpler; decide from the tiny JVM corpus before adding validation.
