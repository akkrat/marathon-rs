## Models, staging and Kotlin defects

## Proposed simplifications (for orchestrator approval, not decided)

Few users: avoid expensive emulation reachable only with exotic inputs. Until approved, baseline parity stands; record approved differences and witness cases in findings.

Preserve normal signed/in-range parsing, trim/defaults, custom errors and all 14/16 gates. Narrow non-ASCII/overflow witnesses explicitly; D9 does not authorize this automatically.
