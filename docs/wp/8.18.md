# WP 8.18: Remove the unused cancellation tags and disposable completion registrations

Effort **L**. Branch `wp/8.18-cancellation-tags-registrations`, based on `main` after WP 8.17 (commit
`424a6388`). WP 8.16 may still be in flight; whichever merges second rebases.

## Why and what

`docs/design/coroutine-quirks.md` (WP 8.17, reviewed) is the specification. This WP implements its first
split item (§5, row 8.18) and its D-CQ1 draft:

1. **Collapse the unused ordinary cancellation-tag distinction** (`Cancellation::Parent` versus
   `Requested`): one ordinary cancellation variant. Keep the `Failure` payload, timeout identity
   (`Timeout { scope, .. }`), failure policy and every place the payload is read. Migrate the Apple/iOS
   payload adapters (for example `marathon-ios/src/device/error.rs`) and the synthetic constructors.
2. **Remove disposable completion registrations:** the public `CompletionRegistration` trait/handle
   (`runtime.rs`), handle construction in `job.rs`, the boxed return of `invoke_on_completion`, DeviceActor's
   stored handle and the contracts test's handle field. Preserve DeviceActor's weak callback retention,
   exactly-once invocation and immediate invocation for a late registration.

One commit per item.

## D-row

Add **D64** to PLAN.md §3.11 from the D-CQ1 draft (same format as D55–D63): rc.1's coroutine adapter
carries an unused `Parent`/`Requested` distinction and disposable completion handles; 8.18 has one ordinary
cancellation and non-disposable callbacks. State that results, worker commands, pinned output and **exit
codes are unchanged**, that the tags never reliably identified the origin (a job signals its own failure
with `Parent`; children inherit `Requested`), and that the public `CompletionRegistration` API is removed
(the workspace is `publish = false`). Add the PLAN.md §7 rows `8.18 | Remove the unused cancellation tags
and disposable completion registrations (docs/wp/8.18.md) | L | 8.17` and
`8.19 | Scheduler pool index, plain job storage and sealed completion (docs/design/coroutine-quirks.md) | M |
8.18`. Keep `scripts/check_plan_dag.py` green.
